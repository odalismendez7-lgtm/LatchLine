# Instructions for AI engineering sessions on LATCHLINE

Read `docs/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md` before doing any work. It is binding.

Non-negotiables:
- $0 budget: no paid services, paid APIs, hosting, domains, billing, or public deployment without explicit founder approval. Say so *before* any action that could cost money or expose the app publicly.
- Never invent business facts, never fake functionality, never claim an action succeeded unless a real result confirms it.
- Never mark a feature done until it is implemented, tested, and reviewed. Never call the product production-ready unless the required checks have passed and are recorded in `docs/TEST_CASES.md`.
- Never commit secrets or customer data (the repo may be public).
- Keep `docs/` current in the same change as the code: `FEATURE_STATUS.md`, `TEST_CASES.md`, `KNOWN_ISSUES.md`, `DECISIONS.md` and others as relevant.
- Do not overwrite or delete existing work without explaining why.
- Work in stages. After each: what was built, files changed, tests run, errors/limitations, next step.
- The founder is not an experienced engineer: explain important decisions plainly.
- Plan prices ($99/$199/$299) are provisional until cost research is done.
