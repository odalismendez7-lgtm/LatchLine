### 7. Emergency Handling

**Suspected gas leak**
- Tell the customer to leave the area.
- Avoid switches, appliances, and ignition sources.
- Contact emergency services or the gas utility from a safe location.

**Carbon monoxide alarm**
- Tell occupants to go outside to fresh air.
- Contact emergency services from a safe location.

**Electrical hazard near water**
- Stay away from the hazard.
- Do not touch electrical equipment or standing water near it.
- Seek appropriate emergency assistance.

**Major flooding or active leak**
- Treat as potentially urgent.
- Follow the approved emergency escalation procedure.
- Never promise technician arrival times.
